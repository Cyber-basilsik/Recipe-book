Roast chicken with lemon

Prep75 min
Servings 4
Rating★★★★★(14)

Ingredients

  • C1.6 kg whole chicken
  • L2 lemons
  • P800 g waxy potatoes

Method

  1. Step 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C. Pat the chicken dry and season it well.

  2. Step 2

    Halve the lemons and push them into the cavity with the thyme.
